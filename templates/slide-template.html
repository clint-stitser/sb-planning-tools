What is S-BOS?

The operating system behind everything we do at Stitser Built.

S-BOS = Stitser Built Operating System
S-BOS is a custom business application that connects our projects, people, data, and AI agent (Kompass) into one unified system. It's not a generic tool — it's built specifically for how Stitser Built works.
What it replaces
Scattered information
Emails, sticky notes, spreadsheets, text threads, and the "I think I saw that somewhere" problem. S-BOS puts everything in a single, searchable, shareable record.
What it enables
Clarity at every level
When every project, deal, and task has a home in S-BOS, you and your team always know what's happening — without having to chase down updates or search through inboxes.

The 5 Pillars

S-BOS is organized around five core areas of the business. Everything has a home.

01
People
Contacts, clients, team members, vendors, and all relationships.
02
Projects
Every active project, deal, and opportunity — with full lifecycle tracking.
03
Finance
Budgets, actuals, pay apps, commissions, and financial reporting.
04
Compliance
Document tracking, required checklists, audits, and sign-off logs.
05
Intelligence
GYR reports, dashboards, Kompass AI, and team-wide visibility.
The rule
If it happened but it isn't in S-BOS, it didn't happen. Every decision, update, and milestone belongs in the system.

How It's Organized

SmartSuite powers the database layer. Google Workspace holds the documents. Kompass bridges both.

Layer 01
SmartSuite
Structured records — projects, contacts, financials, compliance checklists. The system of record for everything that has a proper field and value.
Layer 02
Google Workspace
Documents, contracts, proposals, meeting notes, and photos. Every project has a linked Drive folder for unstructured files.
Layer 03
Kompass AI
Reads both layers. Synthesizes scattered emails and structured records into one answer. Assists with drafts, audits, and summaries.
How to think about it
SmartSuite is where data lives. Google Drive is where documents live. Kompass is the bridge that makes both accessible in plain language — without switching tools.

Your Tools

Three tools, one system. Here's what each one is for and when to use it.

🗄️
SmartSuite
Open and update project records, enter financials, track compliance. Your primary workspace for structured data.
📁
Google Drive
Store and share documents, contracts, and photos. Always linked from the SmartSuite record — never saved loose.
⚡
Kompass
Ask questions, get drafts, run audits. Works in Claude Chat or automated via Cowork. Your AI working partner.
  • Use SmartSuite as your default workspace — check it before checking email
  • If a document doesn't have a SmartSuite record linked, create one
  • Kompass is always available — ask it anything about a project or process

How to Get Help

Four ways to get unstuck — in order of speed.

First — ask Kompass
Chat with your AI partner
Most S-BOS questions — how do I do X, where does Y live, what does this field mean — Kompass can answer instantly. Open Claude and ask in plain language.
Second — check the KB
S-BOS Knowledge Base
This site. Workflow guides, reference articles, and training content are all here. Search for the process name or browse by pillar.
Third — ask your manager
Your direct lead
For decisions, exceptions, or anything that requires judgment beyond what the system tells you. Come with context — your Ops Manager needs the record link, not just the question.
Fourth — flag it
Create a task or note
If something is unclear or broken, log it in SmartSuite as a task so it doesn't get lost. Don't let ambiguity live in your head — put it in the system.
