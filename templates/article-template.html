Understanding the GYR Status Report

GYR stands for Green, Yellow, Red — a weekly health snapshot for every active project. This article explains what the status colors mean, how to read the dashboard, and what it takes to keep your report accurate.

⏱ 10 min read Last updated: May 2026 Pillar: Intelligence

What Is GYR?

GYR is a weekly status signal used across every active project at Stitser Built. It gives leadership, team leads, and project managers a shared, at-a-glance picture of project health without requiring anyone to read through full reports or schedule update calls.

The concept is simple: every project is either on track (Green), needs attention (Yellow), or in trouble (Red). The signal is only useful if it's honest, timely, and specific.

A Green that should be Yellow isn't optimism — it's noise. The GYR system only works when the colors mean something real.

Color Definitions

Color Meaning What It Signals to Leadership
Green On track. No issues requiring escalation. No action needed. Continue monitoring.
Yellow Needs attention. A risk or issue exists — being managed, but visible. Review the note. May need input or a decision.
Red In trouble. Issue is escalated or blocking progress. Immediate attention required. Escalate to leadership.
Important

A Yellow flag with a clear note and a defined action plan is not a failure — it's the system working correctly. The only bad GYR is one that doesn't reflect reality.

The 5 Pillars Explained

Each project's GYR report is broken down by the five S-BOS pillars. This means a project can be Green overall but Yellow on a specific pillar — giving a much more precise picture than a single overall color.

Pillar-Level Status

When you open a project's GYR report in SmartSuite, you'll see a color indicator for each of the five pillars:

  • People — Is the team properly staffed? Are external parties (vendors, partners, clients) responsive and on track?
  • Projects — Is the project on schedule? Are milestones being hit? Are there any blockers to progress?
  • Finance — Is the project within budget? Are there any cost overruns, pending approvals, or unpaid invoices?
  • Compliance — Are all required documents filed? Are there any outstanding compliance items blocking the next phase?
  • Intelligence — Is the S-BOS record current? Has the GYR itself been updated this week?
Pro tip

If you're unsure whether a pillar is Green or Yellow, ask yourself: "Would I be comfortable if leadership reviewed this pillar's data right now, without any additional context from me?" If no, it's Yellow.

Overall vs. Pillar Status

The overall project color is a manual field — it's your judgment call, not an automatic rollup. Even if all five pillars are Green, you might set the overall to Yellow if there's something contextual the colors don't capture (an incoming negotiation, a pending external decision, etc.).

However, the rule of thumb is: the overall color should never be higher than your lowest pillar color. If Finance is Red, the overall should be Red or Yellow — never Green.

How to Read Your Dashboard

The GYR dashboard in SmartSuite aggregates all active projects into a single view organized by status. It's designed for leadership to scan quickly, not read deeply — the detail lives inside each record.

Available Views

  1. Red First view — Sorts all active projects with Red status to the top. Use this as your Monday morning starting point.
  2. By Project Manager — Groups projects by assigned PM. Useful for 1-on-1 reviews.
  3. By Pillar — Shows all projects where a specific pillar is Yellow or Red. Use this to identify systemic issues (e.g., if Finance is Yellow on 6 projects, there may be a process issue, not a project issue).
  4. Timeline view — Shows status history over the last 8 weeks. Identifies projects that have been Yellow for too long.
How Kompass uses this data

Kompass reads the GYR dashboard every Monday and can generate a team pre-read summary — a one-page synthesis of the week's status across all active projects. Ask Kompass to "draft the weekly GYR pre-read" in Claude Chat.

Reading the GYR Notes Field

Each pillar-level color has a corresponding notes field. When a pillar is Yellow or Red, the notes field is required — not optional. A Yellow flag without a note is incomplete and will appear on the compliance audit.

A well-written GYR note has three parts:

  • The issue — What is the specific problem or risk? (One sentence.)
  • The action — What is being done about it, and by whom? (One sentence.)
  • The resolution target — When do you expect this to be resolved or upgraded back to Green? (A date or milestone.)

Finance is Yellow: contractor invoice #1042 ($24K) is disputed and pending resolution. PM is meeting with the GC on 5/30 to agree on a revised amount. Expected to resolve and return to Green by 6/6.

— Example of a well-written Yellow GYR note

How to Update Your Report

GYR reports should be updated every week — the standard cadence is by end of day Monday so leadership can review before Tuesday's team standup. If your project has a major change mid-week, update it immediately — don't wait for the Monday cycle.

Step-by-Step: Updating a GYR Record

  1. Open the project record in SmartSuite
  2. Click into the "GYR Status" section (it's in the right-hand panel on the expanded record view)
  3. Update each pillar color using the dropdown — Green, Yellow, or Red
  4. For any Yellow or Red pillar, update the corresponding note field (issue, action, target date)
  5. Set the overall project color in the main "GYR" field at the top of the record
  6. Save the record — SmartSuite will timestamp the update automatically
Compliance note

If a project's GYR has not been updated in the last 7 days, it will appear as stale in the compliance audit view and trigger a Kompass notification to the assigned PM and their manager.

Using Kompass to Draft Your GYR Note

If you're struggling to articulate a Yellow or Red note clearly, Kompass can help. Open Claude Chat and say:

"Help me write a GYR note for the Finance pillar. The issue is [brief description]. The action we're taking is [what's happening]. The target resolution is [date or milestone]."

Kompass will draft a concise, properly structured note you can copy directly into SmartSuite. It won't submit the note itself — you always review and paste manually.

Questions?

Ask Kompass "how do I update my GYR report?" for a step-by-step walkthrough, or reach out to your Ops Manager if a status situation is genuinely ambiguous.
