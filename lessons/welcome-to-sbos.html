Welcome to S-BOS

S-BOS is the operating system that runs Stitser BUILT — a connected layer of data, intelligence, and process that makes the company scalable, transparent, and fast. This article gives you the foundational orientation you need to work inside it effectively.

⏱ 10 min read Last updated: May 2026 Pillar: People

What Is S-BOS?

S-BOS stands for Stitser Built Operating System. It's not a single app — it's the combination of data infrastructure, AI agents, workflows, and team behaviors that allow Stitser BUILT to operate at scale across multiple product lines, projects, and people simultaneously.

Most companies run on tribal knowledge, scattered spreadsheets, and informal communication. Stitser BUILT is building something different: a system where the right information is always in the right place, the right people always know what to do next, and the organization can learn and improve in real time.

S-BOS is how Stitser BUILT turns operational complexity into competitive advantage.

You don't need to understand every part of S-BOS to be effective in your role — but you do need to understand the shape of it, what's in it, and where your work lives inside it.

The Three Layers

S-BOS is built on three interconnected layers. Each layer serves a different function, and together they create a system that is both powerful and practical.

Data Layer
SmartSuite — the source of truth

Every project, person, property, document, task, milestone, and financial record lives here. SmartSuite is the central database. If it isn't in SmartSuite, it doesn't officially exist in S-BOS.

Agent Layer
Kompass — the AI operating agent

Kompass is S-BOS's AI system, built on Claude. It reads from and writes to SmartSuite, guides workflows, answers questions, drafts documents, and surfaces insights. Kompass is the intelligence layer — it does the thinking work so you can focus on the decision work.

Interface Layer
Softr + Knowledge Base — how people interact with the system

The S-BOS frontend (app.stitserbuilt.com) is built on Softr. It gives every team member a role-appropriate view of data, workflows, training, and tools — no SmartSuite admin access required. This Knowledge Base (where you are now) is also part of the interface layer.

The big idea

The three layers work together: SmartSuite holds the data. Kompass moves and interprets the data. Softr and the Knowledge Base show the data to the right people in the right format. Your job is to keep your part of SmartSuite current — the rest of the system is built around that discipline.

The Five Pillars

Everything in S-BOS is organized around five pillars. The pillars are the lenses through which Stitser BUILT tracks health, makes decisions, and measures progress. Whether you're looking at a project record, a GYR report, or a workflow document, you'll see these five pillars:

👥
People
Team structure, staffing, roles, culture, onboarding, and human performance. Are the right people in the right seats doing the right work?
📋
Projects
Project execution across all product lines — from intake to close. Milestones, schedules, tasks, and status signals live here.
💰
Finance
Budgets, pay apps, invoices, compensation, and financial health across the company. The numbers are in S-BOS, not in someone's head.
✅
Compliance
Contracts, disclosures, licensing, documentation, and regulatory requirements. Nothing slips through the cracks because the system tracks it.
🧠
Intelligence
Data quality, GYR reporting, decision logs, and system health. Intelligence is the meta-pillar — it's about whether the system itself is working and whether the data inside it can be trusted.

You don't need to own all five pillars — most roles touch two or three. But you do need to understand what each pillar means because it's the shared vocabulary across the whole organization.

How It Works Day-to-Day

Working inside S-BOS is more behavioral than technical. The platform is only as good as the discipline behind it — which means S-BOS depends on you updating records, following workflows, and using Kompass as a genuine thinking partner rather than just a search engine.

The Core Habits

  • Keep your SmartSuite records current. If something changed, update it. Don't wait for a weekly review or a reminder. The system's signal quality is a direct reflection of how often records are updated.
  • Use Kompass for the thinking work. Before you draft an email, write a note, create a document, or make a decision — try asking Kompass first. It's trained on every playbook, protocol, and process at Stitser BUILT.
  • Follow the workflows. S-BOS workflows aren't bureaucracy — they're the distilled lessons of everything that's gone wrong (and right) before. When in doubt, there's a workflow for it.
  • Log decisions and context. If you make a decision or take an action that isn't self-evident from the SmartSuite record, add a note. Future you (and your teammates) will thank you.

Where to Start Each Day

Your S-BOS starting point depends on your role, but most team members follow a similar pattern:

  1. Open app.stitserbuilt.com and check your current priorities view
  2. Review any tasks assigned to you or overdue in SmartSuite
  3. Check the GYR dashboard for any projects you're responsible for
  4. Use Kompass (Claude Chat) for anything that requires drafting, research, or judgment
Kompass tip

Start your first Kompass session by saying "I'm [your name], [your role]. What should I be focused on today?" Kompass can pull your current priorities from SmartSuite and give you a personalized starting point.

Your Role in the System

S-BOS is a living system — it gets smarter and more useful the more consistently it's used. As a team member, your role is twofold:

1. Be a good data citizen. Keep your records current. Follow the workflows. Document decisions. Flag problems early. The quality of S-BOS is a direct product of team discipline — and the team depends on you for your part of it.

2. Use the system, don't work around it. When you find yourself solving a problem with a spreadsheet, a text thread, or a "hey can you just send me that file" — that's a signal that the system isn't being used for something it could handle. Surface it. Bring it to Kompass or your team lead. S-BOS is designed to absorb that kind of work.

The goal isn't to make you work harder. It's to make the organization smarter so everyone can work on higher-value things.

Questions and Help

The S-BOS Knowledge Base (this site) is your first stop for how-to articles, workflow guides, and reference material. If you can't find what you need here, ask Kompass — it has access to every workflow, protocol, and playbook in the system.

When Kompass doesn't know

Kompass is trained on documented processes — it doesn't know what isn't written down. If you ask Kompass something and it can't give you a clear answer, that's a signal that the process needs to be documented. Flag it to your team lead or Andi (S-BOS Admin) so it can be captured.

Welcome to S-BOS. The system works when the people inside it work together — and you're now one of those people.
