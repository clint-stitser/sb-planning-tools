Stage 03 — Value Chain

Execute Diligence & Contingencies

Request all diligence documents, initiate contingency timelines, and order inspections — two distinct steps, both critical.

● Audit Required
← Escrow Open Assemble & Respond →
Home→ Workflows→ Execute Diligence
Step 1 of 2
Request Diligence Documents & Initiate Contingency Timelines
Supplies
Production Manager
  • Audit required docs checklist against what's on file
  • Send formal document request to Title and Partner Agent
  • Request timeline updates from all parties
  • Add Practitioner notes to FUB and Smartsheet
Clarify & Verify
⇅
Confirm all requests sent and acknowledged before moving to Step 2
Receives
Title & Escrow
  • Preliminary title report
  • HOA documents
  • CC&Rs
  • Settlement statements
Partner Agent
  • Seller disclosures
  • Inspection reports (if any)
Lender
  • Pre-qualification / 1003
  • Loan status update
Step 2 of 2
Order Inspections
Supplies
Practitioner
  • Confirm client strategy and desired inspection timing
  • Confirm vendor selection and payment method
  • Check all party calendars for availability
  • Confirm utilities are on at property
  • Order inspections with confirmed vendors
Clarify & Verify
⇅
Confirm inspector, date, time, payment, and access are all locked before proceeding
Receives
Partner Agent
  • Confirms property availability for inspection
Escrow / Mortgage
  • Provide payment details for inspection fees
Production Manager
  • Update FUB action plan with inspection details
Outputs
Key Deliverables
  • Document request sent — formal request logged in FUB and Smartsheet
  • All FUB and Smartsheet notes updated with Practitioner guidance
  • Inspector confirmed — name, date, timeframe, payment on file
  • Utilities confirmed on at property
