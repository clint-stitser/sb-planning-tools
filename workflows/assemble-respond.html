Stage 04 — Value Chain

Assemble & Respond

Receive and deliver diligence documents, review with the client, and — if needed — object and provide written substantiation.

← Execute Diligence Re-Inspections →
Home→Workflows→Assemble & Respond
Step 1 of 2
Receive & Deliver Diligence Documents
Supplies
Production Manager
  • Receive reports and invoices from all parties
  • Update Smartsheet tracking with received items
  • File or upload per signature requirements
  • Alert Practitioner: incoming document and its meaning
Clarify & Verify
⇅
Confirm Practitioner received and understands document before client delivery
Receives
Practitioner
  • Review document with client — explain meaning and implications
  • Create NORR if needed
  • Set up Authentisign for client signature
Buyer
  • Review and sign off on documents
Step 2 of 2
Object to Diligence Documents
Supplies
Buyer
  • Decide to reject or require change based on findings
Practitioner
  • Provide written explanation and substantiation of required change
  • Draft NORR with specific requests
Clarify & Verify
⇅
Confirm NORR is complete and substantiation is documented before sending
Receives
Partner Agent
  • Receive NORR
  • Work with seller client for formal response
  • Return accepted, countered, or rejected response
Outputs
Key Deliverables
  • Practitioner alert sent — client notified of incoming document with context
  • Documents reviewed and signed by Buyer
  • NORR drafted and sent if objections exist — with written substantiation
  • Smartsheet tracking updated — all received items logged
