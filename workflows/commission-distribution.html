Stage 08 — Value Chain

Commission Distribution

Review compliance docs, verify commission accuracy, deposit checks from Title, and disburse.

← Closing Nesting →
Home→Workflows→Commission Distribution
Supplies
Ops Manager
  • Review all compliance documents in the closed file
  • Confirm E&O insurance is current
Broker
  • Verify commission amounts are correct against signed instructions
  • Approve disbursement
Accountant
  • Deposit commission checks received from Title
Clarify & Verify
⇅
Confirm check cleared before disbursing — never disburse on a pending deposit
Receives
Accountant
  • Verify check has cleared
  • Disburse commissions per signed instructions
  • Update commission tracking records
  • Update insurance tracking
Outputs
Key Deliverables
  • Commission disbursement complete — all parties paid per signed instructions
  • Receipt of deposit confirmed — check cleared before disbursement
  • Commission tracking updated in SmartSuite
  • Insurance tracking updated
