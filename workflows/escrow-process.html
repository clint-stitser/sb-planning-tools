How to Read This
Three Layers, Every Stage

Every stage in this process is built on the same structure: someone Supplies their value and hands it forward. Something transfers — a document, a decision, a notification. Someone Receives it, clarifies, and verifies before acting. That's the human layer.

Beneath it, two more layers operate. S-BOS holds the record of what happened — the deal file, documents, dates, and status. Claude reads that live data and can assist with audits, drafts, summaries, and flags — at the moment the team needs it, without switching tools. All three layers work together at every stage.

The IREN-MD framework guides every handoff: Information · Request · Emotions · Needs · Materials · Decisions. When you receive something from a team member or external party, run it through IREN-MD before responding. "Clarify and Verify" is not optional — it is the quality gate that keeps the chain intact.

Any Time
Parallel Value Chains

These four situations can arise at any stage. They don't fit neatly into the linear flow — handle them as they arise, always with Clarify & Verify applied, always logging back to S-BOS.

Failed Contract
Deal falls out of escrow at any stage. File must be properly closed, all parties notified, and deal record updated immediately.
  • Practitioner — notify all parties; close FUB action plan
  • Production MGR — close deal record in S-BOS; file all documents
  • Ops MGR — compliance review; archive file
Contract Addenda
New terms, timeline changes, or repairs negotiated at any point during escrow. Fully executed addendum must be in file before terms take effect.
  • Practitioner — negotiate and draft; educate client
  • Production MGR — pursue full execution; update FUB and S-BOS dates
  • Partner Agent — execute and deliver
Deal Help / Problem Solving
Complex situation arises requiring collaborative problem solving — legal question, inspection dispute, financing issue, or negotiation breakdown.
  • Practitioner — bring issue to Production MGR with IREN-MD context
  • Production MGR — coordinate with Broker if needed
  • Broker — case study guidance and resolution strategy
Timeline Changes
Close of escrow date, contingency deadline, or signing date changes. All downstream milestones must be updated simultaneously.
  • Practitioner — confirm change is agreed with all parties
  • Production MGR — update all dates in S-BOS and notify Title, Lender
  • Claude — recalculates all dependent milestones and flags conflicts

Team Expectations
Team Standards
What We Expect from Everyone
  • Guide clear and confident decisions — clients must believe in and trust the process
  • Clarify and Verify at every handoff — do not proceed on assumptions
  • Follow the value chain — each role does their part, in order
  • Great teamwork — support is okay; systemic chain-breaking is not
  • Log everything to S-BOS — if it isn't in the system, it didn't happen
  • If there's a disagreement, reference the value chain first
  • If the chain is missing detail, schedule a face-to-face — no blow-up calls
