Stage 02 — Value Chain

Escrow Open

Open escrow, notify all parties, verify commissions, and confirm every contact knows their role and preferred communication method.

← File & Project Setup Execute Diligence →
Home→ Workflows→ Escrow Open
Supplies
Production Manager
  • Open escrow with Title company
  • Notify all parties — Title, Lender, Partner Agent, clients
  • Verify commissions are correct in escrow instructions
  • Confirm contact info and preferred communication methods for all parties
Clarify & Verify
⇅
Confirm all parties received intro and know next steps before proceeding
Receives
Title / Escrow
  • Receive opening package and instructions
  • Set up internal escrow workflow
  • Reach out to clients re: next steps
Lender
  • Receive transaction intro
  • Set up loan processing workflow
Partner Agent
  • Receive commission verification
  • Coordinate with their client for next steps
Outputs
Key Deliverables
  • Escrow opened — all parties notified and confirmed
  • Commission instructions verified and on file
  • Contact list complete — names, numbers, preferred channels for all parties
  • Key documents distributed — contracts and disclosures to all relevant parties
