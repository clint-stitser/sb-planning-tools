What This Agent Does

The Stitser Way Rituals skill is the team's structured alignment practice, delivered conversationally through Claude. It covers four ritual types: the morning ritual, the evening ritual, the weekly review, and journal stacks. Each is designed to keep the team — and individuals — operating in alignment with the Stitser Way principles.

The conversations are intentionally one question at a time. Claude asks, you answer, and the ritual moves forward at your pace. The goal is genuine reflection and calibration — not just checking boxes.

What it does: Guides you through structured questions for each ritual type. For morning: priorities, intentions, key focus. For evening: what happened, what moved, what's unresolved. For weekly: review against goals, alignment check. For stacks: journal prompts for deeper reflection.

What it does NOT do: It does not read your calendar or task list automatically (the Morning Brief agent handles that). The ritual is intentional — you bring the context, Claude provides the structure.
