Pay App Audit & Lifecycle

Per-invoice PM audit with 7 standard tasks, plus a full pay application lifecycle from budget import through approval.

Home→Agents→Pay App Audit
Capability
What This Agent Does

The Pay App suite covers two related workflows: the per-invoice PM audit and the full pay application lifecycle. The Pay App Audit creates 7 standard project management tasks for each invoice submitted, generates the document package required for sign-off, and tracks sign & return status.

The Pay App Lifecycle handles the broader workflow: loading the Schedule of Values (SOV), initializing a pay period, walking through line items, and processing draws through approval. It connects to the SmartSuite pay app records and keeps the project's financial tracking current.

What it reads: SmartSuite project and budget records, existing pay app history, SOV.

What it creates: SmartSuite tasks (7 per invoice), document packages, pay app records, draw status tracking.

What it does NOT do: It does not approve payments, does not send wire instructions, and does not make lien waiver determinations. Those require human sign-off.

Process
Pay App Audit — Step by Step

  1. Claude identifies the project and the invoice being audited.

  2. Claude creates 7 standard PM audit tasks in SmartSuite — covering invoice review, lien waiver, insurance verification, budget check, approval routing, document filing, and payment tracking.

  3. Claude generates the document package checklist for this invoice.

  4. Claude presents the task list and document requirements for your review.

  5. Claude tracks sign & return status as tasks are completed.

Your Responsibility
What You Still Own
  • Reviewing the invoice for accuracy against the SOV
  • Approving or rejecting each pay app
  • Lien waiver collection and verification
  • Authorizing payment disbursement
FAQ
Tips & Common Questions
What are the 7 standard audit tasks?
Invoice review, lien waiver collection, insurance certificate verification, budget line check, approval routing, document filing, and payment tracking. Claude creates all 7 automatically — assigned and due-dated per your project settings.
Can I run it for multiple invoices at once?
Run it once per invoice — each invoice gets its own set of 7 tasks and its own document package. This keeps the audit trail clean per line item.
