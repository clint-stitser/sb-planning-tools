What This Agent Will Do

The Morning Brief is S-BOS Operation 1 — the first thing you run each morning. It pulls your active deal priorities, GYR (Green/Yellow/Red) status across all projects, today's calendar, and key overdue tasks from Kompass and assembles them into a concise daily brief.

Unlike the Stitser Way morning ritual (which is reflective and conversational), the Morning Brief is operational. It reads live S-BOS data and tells you what the system sees — what's on track, what's at risk, and what needs your attention today.

This agent is currently in development. The trigger phrase and full capability set will be published here at launch.
