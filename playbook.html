Stitser BUILT Operating Playbook
Win the Situations.
Win the Game.
We don't obsess about the scoreboard. We obsess about the situations — the specific, repeatable chunks of every project where execution either compounds or leaks. String enough won situations together and the scoreboard takes care of itself.
"Elite teams don't talk about winning the game. They talk about winning the situations. When you string enough won situations together, the scoreboard takes care of itself." — Clint Stitser
The Five Situations — every project, every product line
The Four Pillars — required at every handoff
01
🧭
Alignment
Everyone knows the purpose, the constraints, and has access to key documentation. No one should have to search for the plan.
02
💰
Budget
The numbers are locked and viable. A project that moves forward with a fuzzy budget is a project that leaks profit before the first shovel hits the ground.
03
📅
Schedule
Time is accounted for. Every situation has a clock. Teams that don't own the schedule become owned by it.
04
🛡
QC & Safety
The quality standard is non-negotiable. It is not a checklist at the end — it's a gate at every handoff that protects the relationship and the margin.
⚡
The Handoff Rule
Before any project moves from one situation to the next, it must pass all four pillars. A fumbled handoff means the person receiving the ball stops running and starts cleaning up a mess — and that is where culture cracks and profit leaks. Kompass + S-BOS exist specifically to make every handoff clean.
