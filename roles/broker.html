Role Guide · Balanced — DISC Profile

Broker

"Spreadsheet Guru" — compliance knowledge, financial oversight, and legal responsibility for every transaction.

Home→Roles→Broker
Profile
Core Competencies
  • Legal compliance — holds ultimate responsibility for every transaction and disclosure
  • Commission oversight — verifies accuracy of every commission disbursement
  • Contract knowledge — the team's resource for contract questions and interpretations
  • Team leadership — sets standards, trains, and maintains Stitser Properties culture
  • Financial acumen — fluent in deal structures, commission math, and entity financials
  • Problem escalation — the final escalation point when a transaction has a complex legal issue
Value Chain
Stage-by-Stage Responsibilities
Stage 06
Pre-Closing — Receives
Sign commission instructions. Final legal review and approval before closing.
Stage 08
Commission Distribution — Supplies
Verify commission amounts are correct against signed instructions. Approve disbursement to Accountant.
Tools
Tools You Own
  • SmartSuite — transaction records and commission data review
  • Softr — entity-level dashboards and performance views
  • Commission tracking records — verified against signed commission instructions before disbursement
Claude
Working with Claude in This Role

The Broker's primary Claude touchpoint is the Underwriting Model for Formation Homes deals, and the Stitser Way Rituals for personal and team alignment. The compliance-focused agents (Compliance Auditor) feed information to the Broker via the Ops Manager — you review findings, not run the audit yourself.
