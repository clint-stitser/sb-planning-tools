S-BOS — Stitser Built Operating System

S-BOS is a custom-built business application designed to serve the Stitser BUILT companies. It's powered by structured databases and automations hosted on SmartSuite, our working data that lives in Google Workspace, and project schedules managed in Smartsheet.

Kompass

Kompass is a custom-built AI agent built on Claude, designed to be everyone's working partner. Its current and future skills and plugins — described throughout this document — are built to significantly reduce the friction in your work, so you can spend more time thinking, competing, and communicating.

An always-available right hand for every person at Stitser BUILT.

One agent. One identity. Many skills. Kompass knows our company and shows up differently depending on who's asking and what they're working on.

Not faster work. Clearer work.

Friction slows everything.

Without context, everyone gets buried in email, texts, docs, and urgency. We're flipping that — every role gets what they need to think, decide, and execute.

It should be the operating system of the whole company.

The morning I got angry at myself

I started 2026 excited. As an asset manager responsible for apartment buildings we co-own and operate on behalf of investors, we'd spent the last couple of years clearing major hurdles — refinances, operations, agency relationships. Our budgets were locked. We had rhythm. I had a beautiful plan for weekly and monthly reviews with the management team.

Then the year started. Audits. New project financing timelines. Easement disputes. Investor calls. The normal chaos of running a business. And the next thing I knew, we were almost done with Q1 and I hadn't done a thorough job of cataloging where we were versus our goals. I was back in reaction mode.

People came to me asking for approvals — rent reductions, capital expenditures, incentive programs. They needed decisions fast. I was being asked to make them without context. I didn't know if those decisions fit the annual plan. I was making million-dollar choices blind.

One decision came across my desk that had real financial implications. I asked a simple question: how does this impact the annual budget?

No one knew.

That question broke something open in me. Not because my team was incompetent — they weren't. They were drowning in the same information scattered across emails, monthly reports, weekly updates, and requests. Nobody had the time or space to synthesize it all.

In that moment of frustration, I did something different. I took all the information from the previous year, year-to-date emails, monthly reports, everything. I gave it to Claude and said: help me see where we really are.

Claude came back with a 32-page report. It synthesized every email, every monthly report, every decision request. It brought context to the decision I was being asked to make. Within minutes — not hours, not days — I went from angry and blind to clear and confident.

That document changed how I showed up as a leader. Instead of a knee-jerk approval, I could say: yes, I'll grant you this tool, but only within these constraints, for this duration, until we hit this milestone. Then we revert.

The shift — from stressed and reactive to calm and strategic — wasn't about me being smarter. It was about friction removal.

The friction problem

Here's what most organizations get wrong about AI: they think it's a productivity hack. Make people faster. Squeeze more output from the same person.

Sure, productivity will improve, but that's not what I'm primarily interested in.

I'm interested in removing friction so that everyone in the organization has what they need to think, communicate, and compete. Not just executives. Not just leaders. Everyone.

Because right now, in most companies, friction is distributed unequally. The person at the top gets an assistant, a briefing, context. Everyone else gets email and urgency.

A lack of friction shouldn't be a perk for executives. It should be the operating system of your entire company.

The friction I'm talking about isn't laziness. It's the gap between intention and execution. It's the hours spent hunting for information instead of using it. It's the decisions made in darkness because no one had time to consolidate context.

AI removes that friction. Not so we can work more. So we can work better. So we can think. So we can communicate clearly. So we can compete.

What we're building

Kompass is an always-available assistant for every person at Stitser BUILT. Not a chatbot. Not a search engine. An actual acting agent that knows our company — our projects, our Manifesto, our data, our cadence — and that shows up differently depending on who is asking and what they're trying to do.

One agent. One identity. Many skills.

We are not building different AIs for different departments. We are building one Kompass — with a single personality, a single set of values, and a single rulebook — and then teaching it the specific skills each role needs.

  • We only have to teach Kompass "how Stitser BUILT thinks" once.
  • When we learn something new, everybody benefits at the same time.
  • When a PM hands off to a Superintendent, the context comes with.
  • The tone, standards, and discipline of the Manifesto apply everywhere.

The archetype we're building

A young, relentless, enterprising, highly educated professional who just landed a job as the right hand to the CEO. This is their shot to make a difference. They're going to make it their life to know everything and clear the path for the principal to be as effective as possible.— The Kompass archetype, S-BOS Agent Guiding Spec

How your data stays safe

Access to execute, not access to expose.— S-BOS Agent Guiding Spec § 9.2

Six categories of data Kompass will never surface — even if asked directly, even if the question is framed cleverly: employee compensation, investor ownership percentages, investor PII, capital table details, attorney-client privileged communications, and personal or medical information.

And every action Kompass takes that modifies data or sends communication gets recorded in a Decision Ledger — a real queryable table in S-BOS that we can audit and roll back from.

Two surfaces — Chat and Cowork

Chat is conversational. You open a session, ask a question, Kompass responds. Nothing happens without you in the room.

Cowork is autonomous. Kompass runs on a schedule or in the background. You set it up once; outputs stage for your review.

Chat is Kompass answering when you ask. Cowork is Kompass doing the work you'd otherwise forget to do.

Where we are and what's next

Kompass is live today in Chat. The agent identity is configured, the connection to S-BOS is working, and we're in the early phase of building out the first Role Skill Packs. Every capability launches in Reactive mode and graduates to Proactive, then Scheduled, as it earns trust.

§ 02

What Kompass works with

Kompass sits on top of two different kinds of information we live with every day — and bridges them for you, so you don't have to.

Scattered
The messy, everyday stuff
Emails, Google Docs, meeting minutes, text threads, handwritten notes, photos from the field. The context lives in conversations and files — not in neat columns.
Structured
The rigid, system-of-record stuff
S-BOS tables, project records, schedules, pay applications, financial data, the Manifesto, the Playbook, the Data Dictionary. Everything that has a proper home in a system.
The point You shouldn't have to remember where something lives to use it. Ask Kompass "what's going on with this project?" — it reads the emails, checks the tables, synthesizes both, and answers you once.

Chat — the conversational surface

Open a session, type a question, get a response. Kompass uses skills — instruction packages that activate based on what the conversation needs.

Use it when

  • "Draft this week's GYR pre-read for Squeeze Inn"
  • "Audit my project file and tell me what's stale"
  • "Help me think through this investor memo"
  • "What's the impact of this change order on forecast?"

Best for

  • Drafting — communications, memos, reports
  • Thinking partner — deal analysis, strategic work
  • Ad-hoc audits — one project, one question
  • Starter packs — what's on my plate today?

Cowork — the autonomous surface

Kompass runs on a schedule or in the background. Uses plugins — bundles of skills plus the connectors and triggers needed to run automatically. Outputs stage for human review; nothing publishes without sign-off.

Use it when

  • Monday-evening GYR drafts for every active project
  • Nightly data sync between Intacct and S-BOS
  • Monthly compliance sweep (insurance certs, lien waivers)
  • Quarterly CRM health check and deduplication

Best for

  • Recurring rhythm — weekly, monthly, trigger-based
  • Cross-system reconciliation
  • Compliance verification at scale
  • Exception reports — tell me what crossed a threshold
For those who'll build these

A skill is a folder containing a SKILL.md file with instructions. One skill, one workflow.

A plugin is a bundle: one or more skills, plus MCP connectors, plus sub-agents, plus slash commands (like /weekly-gyr), packaged as a single installable unit.

Today: skills work in Claude.ai chat on the Team plan. Plugins activate as we stand up the first automated workflows.

§ 04

How your data stays safe

Click any card for detail
"Access to execute, not access to expose."
The governing principle — S-BOS Agent Guiding Spec § 9.2

Six categories of data Kompass will never surface in drafts, reports, or responses — even if asked directly, even if the question is framed cleverly.

01
Employee compensation — salaries, rates, raises, bonuses
02
Investor ownership percentages & capital contributions
03
Investor PII — contacts, tax records, addresses
04
Capital table — equity splits, promotes, waterfalls
05
Attorney-client privileged communications
06
Personal or medical information about any team member

The principle, in practice

A Project Manager building a pay application needs to know the minimum billable rate for a given role. Kompass accesses the underlying salary plus our markup rules, does the math, and surfaces only:

"Minimum billable rate for this role is $X."

The underlying salary is never shown.

The anti-manipulation rule

"That's not information I'm able to share in this context. If you need to work through a compensation or cost structure question, let's define what you actually need to execute — and I'll work from there."

The Decision Ledger

Every action Kompass takes that modifies data or sends communication is recorded in a queryable S-BOS table — who approved it, what options were presented, which was chosen, what the reasoning was, and a link back to the source record.
