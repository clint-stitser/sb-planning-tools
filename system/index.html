What is S-BOS?

S-BOS — the Stitser Business Operating System — is a custom-built business application that runs every deal, project, and transaction across all Stitser BUILT entities. It is the place where the team lives: projects, tasks, compliance records, pay apps, commission tracking, and client files are all in S-BOS. Every team member accesses it directly — it's built around how Stitser operates, not the other way around.

S-BOS connects to Google Drive for document storage, Smartsheet for construction schedules, and Follow Up Boss for client relationship management. Together these tools give the team everything they need without switching contexts unnecessarily.

Claude connects to S-BOS through Kompass, the integration layer that gives Claude read/write access to live data. When a team member invokes a skill — say, "Baseline this project" — Claude is not guessing. It is reading live records from S-BOS, running a defined workflow, and writing structured data back in real time.
